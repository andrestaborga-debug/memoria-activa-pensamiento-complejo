The Diagrams of Method

Original figures from Edgar Morin's Method I: The Nature of Nature
Translated to English · Cátedra 2006 edition · 15 figures

The Tetralogical Loop

p. 74
The cosmogenetic tetralogical loop: disorder, interactions/encounters, order, and organization as co-constitutive principles.

Three Cosmological Models

p. 87
Three cosmological models: (1) the vicious circle of eternal return, (2) diasporic scattering with temporary clumps, (3) the uncertain universe — a catastrophic source feeds organizational loops that leak dispersions.

The Cosmophysical Loop & Spiraloid Circuit

p. 92
Top: the closed tetralogical loop. Bottom: the irreversibly spiraloid circuit — fed by an energetic source (the initial catastrophe), always losing to dispersion.

The Trinitary Concept

p. 126
The trinitary concept: interactions, interrelations, organization, and system — three inseparable yet distinguishable faces of one phenomenon.

Infra ↔ Supra: Texture & Structure

p. 133
The simple hierarchy is replaced by organizational retroactivity: the ultimate product retroacts upon and transforms what produces it.

The Systemic Paradox

p. 135
The apparently absurd double formulation of Jacques Sauvan: S ≷ S. The organizational foundation of the paradox.

The Whole Is More — And Less

p. 138
The vertical hierarchy from emergences (+) down to virtualities (−). Every system must be considered for both its emergent gains and its losses through constraints.

The Water Cycle as Wild Machine

p. 192
The cycle sea → cloud → rain → spring → river: a loop of distinct thermo-hydraulic-aeolian processes, each bound to its own context yet constituting a moment of the cycle.

Genealogy of Machines

p. 204
The Sun as archē-machine branches into clockwork, wild motors/vortices, living poly-machines, the anthropo-social megamachine, and artificial machines.

Openness and Closure

p. 242
Openness and closure are not alternatives but recursive co-producers. The loop is simultaneously opener and closer.

The Production of the Self

p. 244
Production produces a producer that produces it. The recursive circuit of self-production: same → same = re → se = the Self. Identity proceeds from a recursive logic.

Negative & Positive Feedback + S-Curve

p. 254
Negative and positive feedback work in each other's service. All biological growth takes the form of an S-curve.

Yin-Yang: Figure of Complexity

p. 262
The cosmogonic circle — symbolically vortical through the interior S. A figure of complexity, not merely harmony. Vortex and loop co-produce each other.

Active Organization

p. 267
The full inventory of traits that jointly define an active organization: phenomenal description (left) and generative description (right).

Machine ↔ Being ↔ Existence

p. 268
The being-machine relation is one of mutual dependence. Production-of-self designates the central recursion. Or more precisely: a triangle of being, existence, and machine.
